Meet the People Behind Peaks

“Different personalities, specialties, and coffee orders—but one shared love for what we do. We’re creating a welcoming place to laugh, create, and help every client feel comfortable from the moment they walk through the door.”

    Owner & Stylist

    Meet Reyna, owner and stylist at Peaks. With 19 years behind the chair, she specializes in blondes and vivids. Outside the salon, she’s an animal lover, sports mama, and—according to her post—usually has a Starbucks in hand.
